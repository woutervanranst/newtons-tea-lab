🍎 How Newton’s Law of Cooling Works

1. Big Temperature Gap = Fast Cooling!

When boiling 95°C water meets a cool 20°C room, the gap (ΔT = 75°C) is huge, so heat escapes super quickly and the graph drops steeply.

2. Small Gap = Slow Cooling (The Curved Tail!)

As the water gets closer to room temperature, ΔT shrinks, so heat escapes slower and slower. That creates Newton’s famous exponential curve that flattens toward the dashed blue Room Temp line!

3. Isolation Factor Controls “k”

Better insulation traps heat inside the thermos (smaller k), stretching the curve out so your tea stays cozy for hours. Try pinning a run with 📌 Compare and testing 10% vs 90% isolation!

4. Freezing Enthalpy (The Flat 0°C Plateau!)

Set Room Temp below 0°C (down to -20°C)! When water hits 0°C, its temperature pauses completely flat while releasing its enthalpy of fusion (334 kJ/kg) to form ice. Once 100% frozen, solid ice cools about 2× faster!

5. Even Cold Water in a Hot Room Obeys It!

Set Kettle Temp to 25°C and Room Temp to 40°C — the curve flips upward and warms toward room temperature using the exact same equation!
